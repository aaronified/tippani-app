# ADR-0020: The colours are chosen in Settings

- **Status:** Accepted and built; widens [ADR-0005](ADR-0005-Material-You-Only.md). The owner, 9 November 2026: "add app theme colours (android you,
  along with wallpaper colours)".

**Decided.** Material You stays the default and stays Material 3: the phone's own colours, which follow its wallpaper. Settings, Colours also offers the
colours of a photo the reader picks (the dominant colour of the picture is the seed) and a few palettes of the app's own (Tippani red, indigo, teal,
forest, rose, amber, slate). A seed makes the whole light and dark scheme the way Material 3 does, so every screen, the bottom bar and the new icons
follow it.

**Consequences.** The choice is kept on the phone and not synced, since a colour belongs to one screen. The light and dark setting is still the
phone's. The web app's themes, material sets and textures are still not carried over.
